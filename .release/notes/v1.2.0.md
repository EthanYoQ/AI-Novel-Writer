# AI 小说作家 1.2.0

> 发布说明草稿，尚未发布。

## 本次变化

- 全新杂志式写作界面，统一书架、编辑、角色、任务和设置入口，保留字体、主题、缩放及图片外观偏好。
- 蓝图编辑与章节创作使用一致的章节定位选项；旧定位、自定义值和已有空值在未主动修改时保留。感谢 SIRIUS（skywolf123）在 [#309](https://github.com/EthanYoQ/AI-Novel-Writer/pull/309) 中的贡献。
- 生成任务保存执行进度、已收到的内容与恢复依据；中断后可查看候选内容，并在来源仍有效时继续，避免旧任务覆盖较新的编辑。
- 新建正文先自动生成短细纲，再按细纲写稿。中断恢复沿用原细纲；细纲本身不会保存为章节正文。
- 角色使用稳定身份，区分作者设定与定稿派生状态；冲突更新留待确认，恢复副本可继续积累自己的章节进度。
- 写稿上下文优先纳入作者设定和已定稿事实，保留直接前章衔接并报告覆盖缺口。写稿、审稿和修稿保留必需原文及本章目标，按实际模型容量准入；可选材料因预算省略时记录原因，必需原文无法容纳时停止。审稿逐项展示本章目标与正文证据，待核实项由作者决定是否修订。
- 正文超过目标字数 30% 时，在原任务预算内压缩一次。完整压缩稿仍超长时提示“字数超过约定”，保留正文并继续原定章节。修稿相对源稿超长也只提示；原有偏短处理、完整内容和作者事实保护保留。
- 大纲与蓝图的每章字数是生成软目标。结构完整且完成可信的超目标内容保留原文。结构无效或截断时走有限恢复，并保留未完成成果。
- 单次大纲与蓝图规划默认 5 章、每章目标 600，输入目标最多 10 章、每章目标 1000。篇幅按项目写作语言计数，中文按字符数，英文按词数。单次规划不自动遍历全书。完整连续前缀可编辑并直接保存，无需新模型请求；缺章可继续生成。单次章数范围不是整本上限，1000 的篇幅目标也不是输出硬截断。
- 新架构角色提议、新审稿报告和定稿连续性记录保留完整字段内容，避免短字符上限裁掉人物细节、问题说明或句尾否定。
- 蓝图接受完整的长角色名、候选角色名和关系描述。角色身份、关系端点和章节覆盖仍需有效。
- 新正文组合保留不同场景中有意重复的段落；正常中英文小说开头不再被误判为输出说明，明确的修订说明仍会被拒绝。
- 叙事线索生成区分合法空结果与不合格结果；非法、超限或部分非法候选会明确失败，原始输出仍保留。
- 新定向复核接收外围带说明或代码围栏的唯一完整合法报告，保留其中的摘要、理由和引文。
- 世界观等追加续接保留完整原始任务，只限制已生成文本的引用尾部；容量或原任务预算不足时停止，并保留已有部分成果。
- 修复项目创作策略向生成主流程的传递；使用 SiliconFlow 或 OpenAI 服务商标签、OpenAI 协议、`https://api.siliconflow.cn/v1` 和 `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash` 时，可显式选择 high/max 推理档位。
- 通用 OpenAI 协议配置不再依赖内置型号名单。选模使用接口明确资料或已知服务资料，保留作者输出限制；缺少资料时仍可调用和手动设置。思考档位按已知规则转换，预览显示实际发送值。新修稿使用作者本次选择的模型；中断恢复仍沿用该阶段原模型。
- 新建或重置模型配置的默认输出上限为 65536，已有作者显式值保留。实际请求仍受所用模型容量和原任务预算限制，该默认值不表示所有模型都能输出到此长度。
- 官方 DeepSeek 的 `https://api.deepseek.com` 与 `https://api.deepseek.com/v1` 使用一致的能力配置，作者设置的较低输出上限仍然有效。
- 支持完整项目本地归档和手动 WebDAV 云备份。恢复创建独立新副本，保留原项目，并展示备份分支供选择。
- 旧版 v1.0.0、v1.1.0 项目可通过“导入旧项目”复制为新项目，保留原目录；请先保存并关闭旧版及同步程序。
- 知识资料支持查看完整原文和编辑项目内副本；外部原件保持不变，编辑后旧索引停止参与检索，重建索引仍由作者发起。

## 使用边界

- AI 审稿仍可能把前章损失、确认或记账误判为完成当章目标。非逐字引文也可能使目标显示为无法判断。必要的前情回顾也可能被误判为整章复述或没有推进。审稿也可能漏掉作者规定的人物知情时点。跨章事实或同章动作回述有矛盾时，审稿仍可能判为一致。定稿前请核对本章实际行动、代价、知情时点和引文，不要只依赖目标状态。
- 成功完成创作、审稿、修稿、保存和重开，不证明全部 AI 意见正确或长期可靠。已知问题与验证范围见[逐症状验收台账](../../docs/research/novel-quality-modernization/g01-issue-ledger.md)。
- 应用不附带模型或额度，需要配置自己的模型服务。输出仍可能出现事实、时间、人物动机或篇幅偏差，定稿前请核对；不承诺所有模型或所有题材的质量均有提升。质量实验以中文场景为主，英文提示词尚无同等真实实验覆盖。
- 模型列表不一定提供容量和思考参数。未知资料按运行估算处理，不承诺供应商费用硬上限；根预算及未知结果保护继续保留。思考档位不代表不同模型具有相同强度。
- 可选旧章可因预算省略，并报告覆盖缺口；必需原文无法容纳时停止。AI 建议可能有误，确认选择不会使其成为作者事实；修稿后请核对时点与因果。升级前已经开始的批次保留原请求预算。
- WebDAV 为手动完整备份，不是实时同步，也不提供端到端加密；请使用可信服务。系统无法持久保存凭据时会明确使用仅本次会话的凭据。
- 恢复不会重放旧模型任务；新版项目不支持直接交回旧版原地编辑，请保留原件和备份。界面外观设置不等于数据格式降级。
- 已保存的旧角色提议、审稿和复核报告仍按原版本解释，不补写已截掉的文字，也不把旧的“无法判断”改判通过。使用旧正文组合规则的未完成任务仍可能保留旧的去重行为。
- 旧叙事线索已确认项保留原位置与确认记录；混有非法候选的旧结果不能继续采用未确认项。旧任务的模型能力配置发生变化时，执行或恢复会被拒绝，原输出和历史用量保留，不自动重发。
- 有限的审稿保存、重新审稿和重开成功案例不代表历史自然中断已根治；该类中断的根因仍未确定。
- 下载前请查看安装包的签名与公证说明。未签名的 Windows 包可能显示 SmartScreen 或未知发布者提示，组织策略也可能阻止运行。使用 ad hoc 签名且未公证的 macOS 包可能被 Gatekeeper 警告或阻止。

# AI Novel Writer 1.2.0

> Release-notes draft, unpublished.

## Changes

- A magazine-style writing interface brings the library, editor, characters, tasks and settings together while preserving font, theme, zoom and image preferences.
- Blueprint editing and chapter creation use consistent chapter-role options. Existing, custom and empty values remain unchanged unless the author selects a new value. Thanks to SIRIUS (skywolf123) for the contribution in [#309](https://github.com/EthanYoQ/AI-Novel-Writer/pull/309).
- Generation tasks retain progress, received content and recovery evidence. Interrupted work can be inspected and resumed when its sources remain valid, with protection against stale tasks overwriting newer edits.
- New drafts start with an automatic short outline. Drafting and recovery use that outline; the outline itself is not saved as chapter prose.
- Stable character identities distinguish author settings from state derived from finalized chapters. Conflicting updates require confirmation, and restored copies can build their own chapter history.
- Drafting context prioritizes author settings and finalized facts, retains immediate predecessor continuity, and reports coverage gaps. Drafting, review, and revision retain required source text and chapter goals, subject to actual model capacity. Optional material omitted for budget reasons has a recorded reason; requests stop when required material does not fit. Review shows chapter goals with prose evidence; the author decides whether to revise uncertain items.
- A draft more than 30% above its target receives one compression attempt within the existing task budget. If the complete result is still too long, the app shows a notice, retains the prose, and continues the requested chapters. A revision exceeding its source length allowance also receives a notice. Existing handling of short output, content completeness, and author facts remains in place.
- Per-chapter outline and blueprint lengths are soft generation targets. Complete valid output above the target is retained. Invalid or truncated output uses bounded recovery while preserving unfinished work.
- Each outline or blueprint action defaults to five chapters with a target of 600 per chapter. Input targets allow up to ten chapters and a target of 1000 per chapter. Length targets follow the project's writing language, using character counts for Chinese and word counts for English. The action does not automatically traverse the whole book. A complete consecutive prefix can be edited and saved without a new model request, and missing chapters can be generated next. The chapter range is not a whole-book limit, and the length target of 1000 is not a hard output cutoff.
- New architecture character proposals, review reports and finalized continuity records retain complete field content instead of losing character details, explanations or trailing negation to short field limits.
- Blueprints accept complete long character names, proposed character names, and relationship descriptions. Character identities, relationship endpoints, and chapter coverage must remain valid.
- New draft compositions preserve intentional repetition across different scenes. Normal English and Chinese narrative openings are no longer mistaken for model preambles, while explicit revision announcements remain rejected.
- Narrative-thread generation distinguishes valid empty results from invalid output. Invalid, excessive or partially invalid candidates fail explicitly while retaining the raw output.
- New targeted rechecks accept a single complete valid report surrounded by explanatory text or a code fence, preserving its summary, reasons and quotations.
- Append continuations for worldbuilding and related tasks retain the complete original task and only bound the quoted tail of generated text. Existing capacity and task-budget checks still stop requests that do not fit and preserve partial work.
- Fixed project creative strategy propagation into the generation workflow. Explicit high/max reasoning is supported for `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash` at `https://api.siliconflow.cn/v1`, using the OpenAI protocol with either the SiliconFlow or OpenAI provider label.
- Generic OpenAI-compatible configurations no longer require a built-in model entry. Model selection uses explicit endpoint metadata or known service data while preserving the author's output limit; models without this data remain usable and manually configurable. Known reasoning mappings translate the requested level, and the preview shows the value actually sent. New revisions use the model selected for that revision; interrupted stages resume with their original model.
- New or reset model configurations default to a 65536-token output limit while preserving existing explicit author values. Actual requests remain subject to model capacity and the original task budget. This default does not mean every model can produce output of that length.
- The official DeepSeek root endpoint, `https://api.deepseek.com`, and its `/v1` endpoint resolve capabilities consistently while retaining lower author-selected output limits.
- Complete local project archives and manual WebDAV backups restore into independent new copies, preserve the original project and show backup branches for selection.
- Projects from v1.0.0 and v1.1.0 can be copied through "Import legacy project" while preserving their original folders. Save and close the older app and synchronization tools first.
- Knowledge sources support viewing complete originals and editing the project copy. External originals remain unchanged; stale indexes stop participating in retrieval after edits, and rebuilding remains an author action.

## Limitations

- AI review can still mistake predecessor losses, confirmation, or bookkeeping for completion of a current-chapter goal. Non-verbatim quotations can also leave a goal marked as unknown. Necessary references to earlier events can be mistaken for whole-chapter repetition or lack of progress. Review can also miss when an author allows a character to learn a fact. It can incorrectly accept contradictions with prior facts or earlier actions. Before finalization, check the current chapter's actions, costs, knowledge timing, and quotations instead of relying only on goal status.
- Successful drafting, review, revision, saving, and reopening do not validate every AI finding or establish long-term reliability. See the [symptom acceptance ledger](../../docs/research/novel-quality-modernization/g01-issue-ledger.md) for known issues and verification scope.
- No model or usage credits are bundled; configure your own provider. Generated prose may still drift in facts, timing, motivation or length and needs review before finalization. No improvement is promised for every model or genre. Quality experiments primarily cover Chinese scenarios; English prompts lack equivalent real-model coverage.
- Model lists may omit capacity and reasoning metadata. Unknown capabilities use operational estimates, not guaranteed provider billing limits; root budgets and unknown-result safeguards remain active. Reasoning levels do not imply equivalent strength across models.
- Optional older chapters can be omitted for budget reasons, with reported coverage gaps. Requests stop if required source text does not fit. AI suggestions can be wrong; selecting them does not make them author facts. Check timing and causality after revision. Batches started before upgrading retain their original request budgets.
- WebDAV provides manual complete backups, not real-time synchronization or end-to-end encryption. Use a trusted service. When the OS cannot persist credentials, the app explicitly uses session-only credentials.
- Restoring does not replay old model tasks. New-format projects cannot be edited in place by an older app; retain original projects and backups. Changing appearance does not downgrade the data format.
- Saved legacy character proposals and review/recheck reports retain their original interpretation. Previously truncated text is not reconstructed, and historical unknown results are not reclassified as passed. Unfinished runs using the old draft-composition rule may retain its deduplication behavior.
- Saved narrative-thread confirmations retain their original positions and records; remaining items in legacy output containing invalid candidates cannot be newly adopted. A changed model capability configuration can prevent an old run from executing or resuming while preserving its output and usage history, without an automatic resend.
- Limited successful review, re-review and reopen cases do not establish that historical natural connection interruptions have been fixed; their cause remains undetermined.
- Check the package signing and notarization disclosure before downloading. Unsigned Windows packages may trigger SmartScreen or unknown-publisher warnings, and organizational policy may block execution. macOS Gatekeeper may warn about or block packages with ad hoc signing and no notarization.
